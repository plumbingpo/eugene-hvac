How Portland Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
